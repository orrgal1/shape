## Change
We track rev counters but keep no history and can't show what changed. Add:
1. Bridge persists a canonicalized GraphDoc snapshot per revision (canonicalization pattern: `archify/delta/architecture-delta.mjs` — nodes sorted by id, edges by id, stable key order).
2. A pure diff of two snapshots → `{added, removed, changed}` for nodes and edges.
3. Web UI to pick two revisions and render the delta on the existing canvas (added=highlight, removed=ghost, changed=marked) — reuse ReactFlow, do NOT port archify's HTML viewer.

Snapshot storage under the bridge's existing per-project state dir; bound retention (e.g. last 50 revs).

## Acceptance
- After several canvas ops, at least two snapshots exist on disk.
- Selecting rev A vs B in the UI shows correct added/removed/changed sets for a known edit sequence.
- Diff logic covered by a small deterministic test; no project-wide suites/formatters.
