Recommended by builder4 while working #11: shared `applyOps` now has its own smoke (`pnpm smoke:shared`, a54028a), but the bridge's `store.applyCanvasCall` (packages/bridge/src/store.ts) — which merges gate vetoes with shared rejections and interleaves them by op index — has no guard at all.

## Change
Extend the existing bridge smoke (`packages/bridge/scripts/smoke.mjs`, follow its conventions) with checks for `applyCanvasCall`:
- a call mixing gate-vetoed op(s) and shared-rejected op(s) reports both, correctly attributed to their op indexes (the index-interleaving path);
- gate veto receipts and shared rejection receipts both carry the #5 structured shape;
- accepted ops in the same call still apply and bump rev.

## Acceptance
- Bridge smoke passes with the new checks; deliberately swapping two expected indexes fails nonzero.
- No project-wide suites/formatters beyond the bridge smoke itself.

## Result

Batch of 5 ops: gate vetoes at indexes 0/4, shared `op/unknown-parent` at 2 (admitted index 1 — exercises the re-index mapping), accepted at 1/3.
