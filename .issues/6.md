## Change
Our RelationEdge rendering piles multi-edge anchors on node midpoints and has no label collision handling. Vendor the needed pieces of `archify/renderers/shared/geometry.mjs` (1,423 LOC, zero internal imports, MIT — keep dual copyright attribution): at minimum `automaticPortSpread` (deterministic per-side fan-out of edge anchors, sorted by counterpart center), `labelPoint`, and `collectLabelRouteClearance`.

- Wire into packages/web edge rendering after ELK layout so parallel edges into one bubble get spread anchors and edge labels avoid crossing routes.
- Port to TS or keep as vendored .mjs per repo convention — worker's call, one convention only.
